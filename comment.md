Farming practices often follow seasonal cycles that align with different stages of crop or livestock production. As a result, peak usage of agricultural technologies can vary throughout the year, depending on the specific needs of the operation.

A usage-based or “credit per request” model could offer a more financially efficient option for farmers. Farmers can deposit credits into their user cccounts and use them as needed for specific services or features on the platform. This approach allows farmers to pay only for the services they actually use, rather than committing to a fixed subscription fee that may not align with their seasonal needs.
